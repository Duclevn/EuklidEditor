# MDLite privacy statement

MDLite does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts and no automatic update check, and it makes no network requests of its own.

- **Your files and settings stay on your computer.** Settings, the list of open tabs and unsaved-change backups ("hot exit") are kept in your user profile (`%LOCALAPPDATA%`) and are never uploaded.
- **Preview** renders documents offline from files packaged with MDLite. Its page policy blocks network access, including remote images.
- **Terminal** runs the shell you choose (PowerShell or Command Prompt). Commands you type there run with your own permissions and can reach the network like any other shell you use.
- **Links** you choose to open in Preview or Terminal are handed to your default browser after a confirmation.
- Preview and Terminal are displayed with the **Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime**, a Windows component maintained by Microsoft and covered by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). If you install MDLite from the Microsoft Store, the Store's own terms and privacy statement apply to the download and updates.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Duclevn/MDLite/issues.
